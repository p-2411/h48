Duet cards

Every card in the main app, front and back. The front changes with the step a word is on and with what Duet has for it: a moment from a video, a sentence, a sound, a picture. The back is always the same dictionary entry, with only the part that matters at that step brought forward. Every card ends with the same question: Forgot, or Got it.
