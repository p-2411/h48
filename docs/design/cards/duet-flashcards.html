Your words

Notes for building

  1. Cards without a video fill themselves in. A card made by hand or imported has no moment, so its back shows Duet’s example and its front is a word card. The first time the word is heard in a video, Duet attaches that moment (video, time, the line) to the card once, and the card can then show the sentence front and a replay.
  2. “Last heard” is never stored on the card. The card reads it, and the times heard, from the word’s encounter record each time it’s shown, so it stays current without rewriting cards while you watch.
  3. It costs nothing during playback. Duet already matches every subtitle word to its dictionary form and counts it. Finding the word’s card is a lookup by its key (cards already have one, wordKey), done in the background after the line is processed, never on the video’s path.
  4. Collections only appear when there’s more than one. With every card from videos, the Collections view shows By video only.
