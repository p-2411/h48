Good to know

Which languages can I learn?

Japanese, Spanish and Korean, from English.

What do I install?

A desktop app for Mac and Windows. No browser extension.

Which sites does it work on?

YouTube, Netflix, Crunchyroll and Disney+. More after launch.

Does it pause my show?

No. Your show keeps playing while you read, tap a word or answer a question.

How much does it cost?

Early access is free. If that ever changes, you’ll hear it from us first.
