How Duet works

  1. Words you know light up.

    Orange: you know it. Blue: you can say it. Green: you use it. Swipe the subtitles for more Japanese.

  2. Ask about any line.

    Duet explains what just happened and helps you say it yourself. Speak, and it corrects your pronunciation and tone. Ask, and it shows you how it’s said.

  3. Words stick, without pausing.

    At the end of a scene, or a long pause, answer a quick question.

  4. Five minutes a day.

    A short session made from what you watched, your words climbing, and a streak to keep.

“I’m looking for someone.” 探している is 探す (to look for) plus いる: still going on. The の makes it personal.

Can I say this to a friend?

Yes. With friends, 人を探してるの sounds even more natural.

A few things to know.

Which languages can I learn?+

Japanese, Spanish and Korean, from English.

What do I install?+

A desktop app for Mac and Windows. Duet floats on top of your screen, so there is no browser extension to add.

Which sites does it work on?+

YouTube and Netflix first. More after launch.

Does it pause the show?+

No. The show keeps playing while you read, ask or answer. Pause it yourself whenever you like.

How much does it cost?+

Early access is free. We will tell you about pricing before it changes.
