Privacy

The Duet waitlist keeps as little as it can. Here’s what, why, and how to have it removed.

What we keep

When you join the waitlist we store your email address, which sign-up form you used, and when you joined. Nothing else.

Why

To email you when Duet is ready. We don’t sell or share your email, and we won’t sign you up for anything else.

Where it’s kept

In a Postgres database run by Neon, connected to the site through Vercel, which hosts this page.

The page itself

Everything on the page, fonts included, is served from this site. Nothing loads from YouTube, Google or any other service, and there are no cookies. Your browser remembers that you’ve joined, so the form doesn’t ask again; that stays on your device. The scenes shown are from the open film Sintel (© Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0).

Removing your email

Email hello@useduet.io and we’ll delete it. Otherwise we keep it until launch.
