Privacy

The Duet waitlist collects as little as it can. This page says what, why, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we store your email address, which sign-up form you used, and the time you joined. Nothing else.

Why

To email you when Duet is ready and to offer you early access. We do not sell or share your email, and we will not add you to anything else.

Where it is kept

In a Postgres database run by Neon, connected to the site through Vercel, which hosts this page.

The video on the page

The page plays a muted scene from the open film Sintel (© Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0), served from this site. Nothing on the page loads from YouTube or any other service, and the page sets no cookies.

Removing your email

Ask us to delete it at any time by emailing parhamsepas@gmail.com. We keep it until launch, or until you ask us to remove it.
